Privacy

marcinfracz.ai · Updated November 2026

What this site measures

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use it, like which pages get read and where people click. Clarity may record anonymized mouse movements and scrolls.

What it doesn't

No names, emails or form entries are collected, and nothing is sold.

Your choice

Visitors in Europe and the UK are asked first. You can opt out by declining cookies or using your browser's tracking protection.

Microsoft's own privacy statement covers how Clarity handles data: privacy.microsoft.com.
