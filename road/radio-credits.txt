Drive mode radio: credits and licences (round 4, Nov 21, 2026)

Music
- Every song on Night Shift FM, Low Gear, Curbside 97, Redline Rock and Radio Carretera is original.
  The music is composed and played live in the visitor's browser by road/radio-r4.js (Web Audio synthesis).
  No recordings, samples or third-party tracks are used, so there is nothing to license.
- Song titles and artist names are fictional.

Talk, ads, DJ lines and station IDs
- Scripts are original, written for this site (road/radio-r4.js).
- Hosts, callers, DJs and advertised companies are fictional.
- Voices are the visitor's own browser speech voices (Web Speech API). No voice recordings are shipped.

Not used
- No copyrighted songs, and no station names, DJs, songs or branding from any video game.
