Before You Go

Fill the free days, pack the bags

The free days have no bookings in Tatiana's itinerary, so these are ideas, not plans — and a checklist that saves itself on this device.

🇪🇸 Barcelona ideas

Ideas for Mar 26–27 — nothing booked yet.

  • Sagrada Família — book timed tickets ahead, it sells out
  • Gothic Quarter — wander from Plaça de Catalunya, ten minutes from the apartment
  • Park Güell — Gaudí, views, and a good long walk
  • La Boqueria — lunch at the market on La Rambla
  • Barceloneta beach — late March is jacket weather, but the walk is worth it

🇬🇧 London ideas

Ideas for Mar 29–30 — nothing booked yet.

  • Westminster & Big Ben — literally across the bridge from the hotel
  • London Eye — next door; book a timed slot
  • Tower of London — half a day, worth every minute
  • Borough Market — lunch, then walk the South Bank back
  • Hyde Park & Kensington — an easy slow morning
